Nucleus

The open infrastructure for synthetic cell engineering.

Building a cell from scratch takes more than any one lab can do alone. The Nucleus Distribution brings together the protocols, materials, and software the synthetic cell community needs to work as one network: validated specifications live in Docs, new results get shared as DevNotes, day-to-day design and analysis happens in the Hub, and the community works through problems together in the Forum. See how it all fits together in the Nucleus Development Cycle.
