HOW NUCLEUS OPERATIONALIZES COMPOSITIONAL BIOLOGY

Synthetic cell biology advances through reusable components — modules that can be combined, extended, and benchmarked across labs. But the field's incentive structure rewards scientific stories: the novel finding, the paper, the result. The method that produced it is typically buried in a supplementary section, written once and never maintained. Nucleus runs a parallel track. Every piece of experimental work that enters the ecosystem is structured into a Module specification — a reusable, benchmarked spec sheet that answers exactly two questions: what is it, and what should you expect when you implement it. These specs live alongside the scientific narrative, not inside it. The community can engage with specs directly — comment, benchmark against standard methods, extend with new implementations. This is what makes the knowledge compound rather than accumulate.
